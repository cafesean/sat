1. Begin by reviewing the relevant business requirements, customer needs, or market opportunities that drive the feature or improvement. Reference or prepare specifications in the `/spec` folder.

2. Clarify user stories, acceptance criteria, and expected outcomes. If needed, ask the technical team clarifying questions to ensure feasibility and alignment.

3. Once the scope is well understood, write or update a clear, detailed specification in markdown. Include business logic, key constraints, and links to any supporting research or materials. Use Mermaid diagrams if they help communicate user flows or logic paths.

4. Share the specification with the technical team for feedback and refinement. Be open to discussion and iteration to reach shared understanding.

5. Once the team agrees the spec is clear, finalize it in the `/spec` folder using the naming convention agreed upon (e.g., `EPIC-`, `FEAT-`, `SPRINT-`) that represents a clear "code" for that epic, feature, or sprint including an incremented number and a 10 char or less description.

6. Once the specification is finalized, use the `switch_mode` tool to pass it to the 'tech-lead' role. The Tech Lead is responsible for creating technical solution documents that break the spec into implementation-ready stories and tasks.
